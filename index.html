Reconciled Financial Architecture

Compare status-quo operations against automation-first and full AI transformation across 7 independent financial dimensions.

Executive Scenario Reconciliation Table

Side-by-side reconciliation of gross operating savings, technology expenses, net benefits, ROI, and payback.

Financial Benefit Classification & Value Realization Framework
1. Actual Cash Savings Direct outlays eliminated from accounts (e.g. avoided duplicate vendor payments, zeroed penalties, captured discounts). For workflows with internal errors and rework, direct disbursements are 0.
2. Avoided Process Leakage Eliminated defect waste, transaction exception triaging, and delayed queue friction across internal operations.
3. Recoverable Capacity Value Employee hours liberated from repetitive tasks, valued at loaded wage rates. Liberated for customer service, analysis, and strategic execution.
4. Total Economic Benefit Comprehensive gross operating savings combining liberated capacity value and avoided leakage (with actual cash savings embedded, preventing double counting).
Gross Annual Operating Savings Capacity Value Released + Total Avoided Leakage. Total operational value generated before technology expenses.
Annual Technology Opex Ongoing software licenses, cloud capacity, overage fees, and vendor maintenance/support.
Net Annual Recurring Benefit Gross Operating Savings minus Annual Technology Operating Costs. Ongoing annual surplus.
One-Time Capex Upfront implementation, systems integration, workflow automation, and staff training outlay.
First-Year Net Benefit Net Annual Recurring Benefit minus One-Time Implementation Capex. Net value delivered in Year 1.
ROI Metrics (Recurring vs 1st-Year) Recurring Annual ROI: (Net Recurring Benefit / Capex) × 100%.
1st-Year Net ROI: (1st-Year Net Benefit / Capex) × 100%.
Payback Period (Months) One-Time Capex / (Net Annual Recurring Benefit / 12). Indicates elapsed months to recoup upfront outlay. (Scenario A Baseline shows N/A – Baseline Scenario).

Capacity Recovery Distinction: Recovered employee capacity reflects productive bandwidth liberated for high-value execution. It does not constitute guaranteed cash payroll reduction unless staffing or overtime is formally restructured.

Anti-Double-Counting Safeguards: Overlap between transaction error remediation and manual rework hours has been deduplicated. Delayed transactions and SLA penalties are reconciled to prevent double-counting.

About ProcessIQ

Enterprise SaaS Architecture

ProcessIQ was designed to solve a critical enterprise problem: organizations rushing into generative AI licenses without first understanding whether their workflows are standardized, repeatable, or financially suited for automation. By unifying manual labor costs, operational defect leakage, 15-dimensional AI readiness scoring, and transparent vendor total cost of ownership (TCO) models, ProcessIQ equips executives with defensible business cases.

Client-side analytical cockpit • Zero server storage • Pure browser execution Created by Yuvraj Malpure

Client-Side Privacy Guarantee (Zero Database)

ProcessIQ operates exclusively in client memory. All calculations, chart projections, CSV compilations, and PDF renderings are computed locally in your browser. No financial inputs, process parameters, employee counts, or wage data are ever stored in a database, sent across external APIs, or shared with third-party tracking platforms.
